Personal coaching tailored to your current level and goals. Sessions focus on technique, movement, tactical awareness and match play. Suitable for beginners through to advanced players looking to improve specific areas of their game.

Event Based Training Program

Got a major event coming up that you want to do well in? We'll use these 45 minutes to create a tailored training program aimed at helping you peak for the event. You will walk away with a detailed, tailored training program.

Psychological Preparation

›

So much of squash is about psychological preparation. This training module is designed predominantly for teams. I take you through the 4 to 5 psychological tools that I developed to help Paul Coll take on the world. These practical techniques will help you in any competitive situation.

Team Training

›

Designed for interclub teams or any team preparing for a major event. We'll equip you with tailored training programs and team practice exercises that will lift all of your playing levels. We use two courts and I move between the courts advising and moving you through different routines.

Fitness Training

›

You'll love this fitness training. Squash has specific types of movement that we will focus on here. You won't be just running around doing cardio. The training is designed to address both strength and flexibility. Better movement means better squash and this focused training will set you firmly on the path to improvement.

Pairs Training

›

This is where it gets interesting. Training with two similar level players is the ideal. The practice routines are better, you get more flow and both players can feed off each other. Most of all it gets the price down. If you are in doubt about whether your game will get the attention it deserves, don't be. You will also go away with pairs training routines that will lift both of your games.
